Beacon

A judgement-free guide for single parents in Singapore: find out what support may apply to you and your child, and what to do next.

💬 Tap the chat button at the bottom right of the screen and say "hi" to start. You don't need to share your name or NRIC, and you can skip any question.

Beacon can

  • Explain support schemes in simple language
  • Suggest schemes you may be eligible for
  • Tell you what documents to prepare and where to apply
  • Explain what changes from April 2027
  • Point you to a Social Service Office or Family Service Centre

Beacon can't

  • Decide whether you are eligible
  • Apply for schemes on your behalf
  • Give legal, medical or tax advice
  • Replace a social worker
⚠ In an emergency: Police 999 · Ambulance 995 · ComCare Hotline 1800-222-0000 · NAVH (violence or abuse) 1800-777-0000 · Samaritans of Singapore 1767

Beacon is a student prototype built for COR2221 AI & Humanity at Singapore Management University. Information is based on official sources checked in November 2026, but rules can change, so please always confirm with the relevant agency.
