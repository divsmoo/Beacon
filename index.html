Support for single parents, explained simply.

Find out what help may be available for you and your child in Singapore, and what to do next. No name or NRIC needed, and you can skip any question.

What you can ask about

  • Money ComCare assistance, the Child Development Account (CDA) and the MediSave Grant for Newborns
  • Childcare Preschool and infant care subsidies, and KidSTART for children up to age 6
  • Housing HDB rental and purchase options, and how appeals work
  • April 2027 changes What the new SG Child Support Package means for you, and what to prepare now

Good to know

Beacon explains schemes and next steps using official information. It can't decide if you are eligible, apply for you, or give legal or medical advice. For your own case, a Social Service Office or Family Service Centre can help.
